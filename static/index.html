    Target while recording: speech around −20 LUFS on each mic, peaks under −6 dBFS, never red.
        The finished episode is mastered to −16 LUFS / −1 dBTP afterwards.
    
    
    

    
    
    
      
    Mix & master
    
      
    Sawti levels each speaker, closes mics that only carry someone else's voice, mixes, then masters
        to the house standard. Originals are preserved; each job writes _mix.wav and _master.wav files in a new Sawti-Export folder.
