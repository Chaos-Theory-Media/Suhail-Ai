SAWTI 3.0 — studio agents for IMI podcasts

1. Move this folder into Documents and keep it there.
2. Double-click INSTALL_SAWTI.command.
   macOS blocks it the first time: open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
   scroll down, click "Open Anyway" next to INSTALL_SAWTI, and enter the Mac password.
3. When the installer finishes, open "Sawti" from the Desktop.
4. Switch Sawti ON and follow the tabs: 1 Prepare > 2 Pre-test > 3 Live > 4 After the show.
5. To close Sawti, press Quit on the Sawti page.

To practise without the studio: SIMULATE_SAWTI.command
The full guide is in README.md. Notes for IT are in TECHNICAL.md.

CLEAN UNINSTALL (3.0.2): Open Uninstall Sawti from the Desktop/Applications, or
UNINSTALL_SAWTI.command in this folder. Stop recording first. Review components
and paths; recordings and shared tools are kept. Removable files go to Trash.
Read audit/uninstall/README.md for the remaining Mac validation requirements.
